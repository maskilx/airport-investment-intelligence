### Conclusions

- **Build the MVP reference layer from OurAirports only:** `airports.csv` and `runways.csv` are public domain, nightly, and enough for codes, coordinates, and runway length. Derive long-haul share only once a route list exists. https://ourairports.com/data/
- **There is no verified free feed of forward seats by route.** OpenSky, ADS-B Exchange, OurAirports, ACI, and A4A do not sell or give that. Aviationstack’s free plan excludes future schedules and shows no seats. https://aviationstack.com/pricing — https://docs.apilayer.com/aviationstack/docs/api-documentation
- **OAG is the data you eventually want** (seats by class, 12 months forward) and the one you cannot turn on in a day: price is not public, and the license forbids a substitute database or raw resale. https://www.oag.com/airline-schedules-data — https://developers.oag.com/terms-and-conditions
- **Cirium matches OAG on schedules (~11 months) but airport- and route-level schedule calls are contract-only**; the 14- or 30-day trials are too small for a national panel, and public list prices were not found. https://developer.cirium.com/apis/data/overview — https://developer.cirium.com/apis/flightstats-apis/schedules
- **FlightAware is the only vendor here with a public per-call price and cabin-seat fields** (`GET /schedules` = $0.020 per 15 rows, seats nullable, 1-year horizon, 3-week window). A B2B investment product needs Premium at **$1,000/month minimum** before queries. https://www.flightaware.com/commercial/aeroapi/
- **If the budget is about $10–$50 and the scope is a few airports’ near-term boards, delays, and runways, AeroDataBox Pro ($8) is the practical paid add-on.** Do not treat it as a seat-capacity warehouse: seats were not verified in the API, and cheap plans allow only a 7-day cache. https://aerodatabox.com/pricing
- **Aviationstack Basic ($49.99) buys future timetables and aircraft type, not documented seat counts**, and 10,000 calls will not cover a US airport panel for long. https://aviationstack.com/pricing
- **ADS-B feeds (OpenSky, ADS-B Exchange) answer “who is airborne,” not “what capacity is scheduled.”** Both block commercial use on the free/cheap plans. https://openskynetwork.github.io/opensky-api/ — https://www.adsbexchange.com/community/developer-hub/
- **ACI is realized passenger/movement history at four-figure prices and an internal-use license**, not an operating-schedule or delay source. https://store.aci.aero/product/annual-world-airport-traffic-dataset-2026/ — https://store.aci.aero/terms-of-use/
- **A4A restates BTS industry totals.** Useful as a citation for national context; useless for airport-level forward seats. The free historical seats-by-route file is federal T-100, lagged months, and it is not forward. https://airlines.org/dataset/annual-results-u-s-airlines-2/
