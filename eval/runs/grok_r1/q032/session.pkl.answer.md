**Methodology**
- `screen_score:1.0`: Σ weight × anchored score of: Projected passenger increase (FAA TAF FY2025→FY2035, absolute) [10000→10000000, log10] w=1.0; Realized passenger growth (T-100, trailing 12 months vs prior 12) [-0.05→0.1, linear] w=0.0; Home-CBSA per-capita income (latest BEA year) [45000→110000, linear] w=0.0
- `top_carrier_share`: max over operating carriers of seat share, departing passenger/combi segments
- `abs_change:taf_enplanements`: change of [FAA Terminal Area Forecast total enplanements (domestic + international)]: FY2035 vs FY2025
- `pct_change:passengers`: change (%) of [Σ passengers on departing passenger/combi segments]: 2025-08…2026-07 vs 2024-08…2025-07
- `cbsa_per_capita_income`: BEA personal income / population of the home CBSA
- `acais_enplanements`: FAA official calendar-year revenue passenger boardings
- `trend_slope:avg_dep_delay_min`: OLS slope of monthly avg_dep_delay_min, 2025-09…2026-08
- `avg_dep_delay_min`: Σ departure delay minutes (early = 0) / operated departures
- `derived:difference`: difference of [Average departure delay — month=2026-08 — 2025-09…2026-08] , [Average departure delay — month=2025-09 — 2025-09…2026-08]
- `taf_enplanements`: FAA Terminal Area Forecast total enplanements (domestic + international)
- `passengers`: Σ passengers on departing passenger/combi segments

**Caveats and limitations**
- Screening rank of expansion opportunity, not a financial valuation: no costs, debt, airline-agreement economics or construction risk are modelled.
- TAF component is an FAA forecast (unconstrained).
- T-100 carriers are operating carriers; regional affiliates appear separately from their mainline partner.
- FAA TAF values for forecast years are FAA's unconstrained forecast, not observations.
- Federal fiscal year (Oct–Sep).
- T-100 passengers are segment enplanements (connecting passengers count at each enplanement).
- Home CBSA is an administrative proxy for the market area, not a measured catchment.
- Area-level value (home CBSA/county): airports or groups sharing an area repeat the same value; never add these across rows.
- Monthly series include seasonality; slope is descriptive.
- On-Time covers domestic flights of reporting carriers only (no international, no small carriers).
- The screen ranks expansion opportunity. It is not a valuation. Costs, debt, airline-agreement economics, and construction risk are not modelled.
- The passenger-increase component is an unconstrained FAA forecast. It is not observed traffic and it is not unmet demand.
- Terminal capacity, fares, and profitability are not measured by these series.
- Every scored airport is flagged AIRFIELD_UNKNOWN, so a verified airfield capacity constraint is not established.
- Average departure delay covers domestic flights of reporting carriers only. International flights and non-reporting carriers are outside the series. Early departures do not reduce the delay total.
- The monthly slope includes seasonality and is descriptive.
- Airports below the enplanement gate, and airports with no FAA enplanement record, were not scored. Leaving them out is not a low rank.
- A tie flag compares only the previous score. A chain of flags is not a mutual tie, and a flag does not mean the scores are equal.
- The order is specific to demand_only weights. The other scenario columns in the same screen order different airports first.
