**Methodology**
- `freight_lbs`: Σ freight on departing segments
- `rank_score`: Σ weight × minmax_in_set-scaled criterion; criteria: freight_lbs (higher_better, w=1.0)
- `all_departures`: Σ departures performed, all aircraft configurations
- `derived:ratio`: ratio of [All departures (incl. cargo) — ANC — 2025-08…2026-07] , [Passenger departures — ANC — 2025-08…2026-07]
- `derived:difference`: difference of [ratio(F3388, F3508)] , [ratio(F3278, F3398)]
- `trend_slope:all_departures`: OLS slope of monthly all_departures, 2025-08…2026-07
- `trend_slope:departures`: OLS slope of monthly departures, 2025-08…2026-07
- `exploratory_sql`: column freight_lbs of: SELECT d.current_code AS code, sum(s.freight) AS freight_lbs FROM v_t100_segment s JOIN dim_airport d ON d.airport_id = s.origin_airport_id WHERE s.year = 2025 AND d.country = 'US' GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY freight_lbs DESC LIMIT 15
- `departures`: Σ departures performed, passenger/combi aircraft

**Caveats and limitations**
- Scores are relative to the airports in this set; adding/removing airports changes them.
- Monthly series include seasonality; slope is descriptive.
- Exploratory SQL: definitions are not catalog metrics; verify before relying on it.
- Unmet demand, terminal capacity, fares, and profitability are not measured here and are not implied by freight rank or by the departure ratio.
- This is not the multi-factor investment screen, so scenario weights, gates, and concentration flags are not part of the result, and it is not a valuation.
- The set-relative score would change if airports were added or removed. Equal freight is still given sequential positions rather than a shared rank.
- RVR and AFE were inside the ranked set but had no freight-pounds result, so they were not scored.
- Airports with no recorded enplanements were outside the catalog rank because the full US directory exceeds the analysis size limit. The exploratory T-100 query is the check on that gap, and it is labelled exploratory.
- Freight is departing-segment pounds, not landed weight and not cargo revenue. The departure ratio is not cargo pounds per flight.
- The freight window and the T12 departure window are different. Only part of CY 2025 sits inside the T12 window.
- Monthly slopes include seasonality and describe departure counts, not the ratio. No fitted slope of the ratio was calculated.
- Passenger departures cover passenger, combi, and seaplane configurations only. Other configurations, including freight aircraft, sit in all departures and outside that denominator.
