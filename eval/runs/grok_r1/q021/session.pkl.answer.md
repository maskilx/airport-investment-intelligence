**Caveats and limitations**
- Scores are relative to the airports in this set; adding/removing airports changes them.
- Freight enplaned is the sum of freight on departing T-100 segments. It excludes mail. It is not deplaned freight, not cargo revenue, and not ton-miles.
- Unmet demand, terminal capacity, cargo-facility capacity, fares, and profitability are not measured by this ranking.
- Scores are relative to the airports in this set and change if airports are added or removed.
- The only equal observed freight totals are CLD and CCR. Their consecutive positions are a tie, not a freight difference.
- Airports under the commercial enplanement floor are not ranked, even if they enplane freight.
