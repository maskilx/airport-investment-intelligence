**Methodology**
- `cbsa_per_capita_income`: BEA personal income / population of the home CBSA
- `seats_per_departure`: Σ seats / Σ passenger departures

**Caveats and limitations**
- Home CBSA is an administrative proxy for the market area, not a measured catchment.
- Area-level value (home CBSA/county): airports or groups sharing an area repeat the same value; never add these across rows.
- Aircraft upgauging as a change in gauge over time is not measured. No prior-window comparison or CAGR was calculated. The ranking is the level of average seats per departure.
- The rank does not apply a departure-volume gate. Gauge is total seats divided by passenger departures, so a thin passenger schedule can place an airport high in the order.
- Terminal capacity, unmet demand, fares, and profitability are not measured by these data.
- Displayed gauge figures are rounded. Airports can show the same displayed gauge and still be ordered by different unrounded values. The exact calculated tie is DIK, EAR, and PIR.
- Home-CBSA per-capita income is an area value and an administrative market-area proxy. Airports that share a CBSA repeat it. Do not add those figures across airports.
- Commercial airports with no returned home-CBSA per-capita income for the income year are named in the body. The cutoff cannot be applied to them, and the reason each value is missing is not established.
- This ranking is not the investment screen. It does not produce screening flags, and it is not a valuation.
