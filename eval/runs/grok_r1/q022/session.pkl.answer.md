## BNA seats offered increased 8.2% from calendar 2024 to calendar 2025

At Nashville International Airport (BNA), observed seats offered — seats on departing passenger and combi segments — were 15.30M seats in 2024-01…2024-12 (observed) and 16.56M seats in 2025-01…2025-12 (observed). The full-year change is 8.2% (calculated), defined as change (%) of [Σ seats on departing passenger/combi segments]: 2025-01…2025-12 vs 2024-01…2024-12.

The year-over-year percentage trend is the calculated same-month change in those observed seats, 2025 versus 2024: January -0.4%, February -3.4%, March 4.6%, April 7.5%, May 10.1%, June 9.7%, July 8.3%, August 8.9%, September 10.4%, October 13.4%, November 11.8%, and December 13.8%.

A linear fit to the monthly seat levels over 2024-01…2025-12 has slope 12,504 seats per month (calculated). That slope includes seasonality and is descriptive; it is not a percentage and it is not seasonally adjusted. The trend operator's last-month year-over-year change, 2025-12 vs 2024-12, is 13.8% (calculated). December's same-month change is 13.8%. Endpoint levels are Seats offered — month=2024-01 — 2024-01…2025-12 at 1.06M seats and Seats offered — month=2025-12 — 2024-01…2025-12 at 1.37M seats. Those months are different seasons, so the gap between them is not a year-over-year rate.

**Methodology**
- `pct_change:seats`: change (%) of [Σ seats on departing passenger/combi segments]: 2025-01…2025-12 vs 2024-01…2024-12
- `seats`: Σ seats on departing passenger/combi segments
- `derived:pct_difference`: pct_difference of [Seats offered — month=2025-01 — 2024-01…2025-12] , [Seats offered — month=2024-01 — 2024-01…2025-12]
- `trend_slope:seats`: OLS slope of monthly seats, 2024-01…2025-12
- `yoy:seats`: 2025-12 vs 2024-12

**Caveats and limitations**
- Monthly series include seasonality; slope is descriptive.
- Seats offered are departing passenger and combi capacity. Enplanements, seat factor, terminal capacity, unmet demand, fares, and profitability are not measured here and are not established.
- The positive slope of monthly seat levels includes seasonality. It does not mean every month was above the year-ago month; January and February were below.
- The result pools all carriers and all destinations. No carrier, international, or route split is included.
- T-100 departing-seat coverage in the catalog runs from January 2024 through July 2026, so both calendar years in this comparison are complete. This is a capacity trend, not a screening rank and not a valuation.
