**Methodology**
- `screen_score:1.0`: Σ weight × anchored score of: Projected passenger increase (FAA TAF FY2025→FY2035, absolute) [10000→10000000, log10] w=0.3; Realized passenger growth (T-100, trailing 12 months vs prior 12) [-0.05→0.1, linear] w=0.5; Home-CBSA per-capita income (latest BEA year) [45000→110000, linear] w=0.2
- `abs_change:taf_enplanements`: change of [FAA Terminal Area Forecast total enplanements (domestic + international)]: FY2035 vs FY2025
- `pct_change:passengers`: change (%) of [Σ passengers on departing passenger/combi segments]: 2025-08…2026-07 vs 2024-08…2025-07
- `cbsa_per_capita_income`: BEA personal income / population of the home CBSA
- `top_carrier_share`: max over operating carriers of seat share, departing passenger/combi segments
- `acais_enplanements`: FAA official calendar-year revenue passenger boardings
- `npias_need_usd`: NPIAS 2025-2029 development estimate
- `grants_awarded_usd`: Σ awarded FAA grant totals (award date present)
- `taf_enplanements`: FAA Terminal Area Forecast total enplanements (domestic + international)
- `passengers`: Σ passengers on departing passenger/combi segments

**Caveats and limitations**
- Screening rank of expansion opportunity, not a financial valuation: no costs, debt, airline-agreement economics or construction risk are modelled.
- TAF component is an FAA forecast (unconstrained).
- FAA TAF values for forecast years are FAA's unconstrained forecast, not observations.
- Federal fiscal year (Oct–Sep).
- T-100 passengers are segment enplanements (connecting passengers count at each enplanement).
- Home CBSA is an administrative proxy for the market area, not a measured catchment.
- Area-level value (home CBSA/county): airports or groups sharing an area repeat the same value; never add these across rows.
- T-100 carriers are operating carriers; regional affiliates appear separately from their mainline partner.
- NPIAS figures are sponsor-reported 5-year AIP-eligible NEED estimates, not funding.
- Awarded grant dollars at award date; not spending or project completion.
- The screen does not model costs, debt, airline-agreement economics, or construction risk.
- The enplanement-change term is an unconstrained FAA forecast. Realized growth is recent passenger change, not a projection.
- Terminal capacity, unmet demand, fares, and profitability are not measured, so they cannot be read from the rank.
- No scored airport has a verified FAA capacity rate, so airfield feasibility is not established.
- Tie flags compare each score only with the previous rank under the model's tie rule. They do not mean the scores are equal, and a chain of flags is not a single mutual tie.
- NPIAS need is an estimate and grants awarded are observed dollars. Neither enters the score. Carrier concentration is a flag, not a score penalty.
- Smaller commercial airports below the gate, including the first listed airport FWA, were not ranked. Airports with no FAA enplanement record were not ranked either.
