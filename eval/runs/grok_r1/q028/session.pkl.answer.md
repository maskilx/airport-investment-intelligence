## Covered months only: DFW leads West South Central airports on scheduled peak-hour intensity

A rank for the full requested calendar year cannot be established. The scheduled peak-hour metric comes from On-Time data, which does not cover January through August of that year. The ranking below uses the months of that year that are covered, September through December, and only airports in the West South Central census division that have this series.

From highest to lowest observed scheduled peak hour (arrivals plus departures, reporting carriers, operated flights): DFW 135 operations/hour; IAH 58 operations/hour; AUS 38 operations/hour; DAL 30 operations/hour; HOU 26 operations/hour; MSY 24 operations/hour; SAT 18 operations/hour; OKC 11 operations/hour. ELP 9 operations/hour ties TUL 9 operations/hour. XNA 7 operations/hour ties LIT 7 operations/hour. Next is MAF 5 operations/hour. LBB 4 operations/hour ties SHV 4 operations/hour. Next is BTR 3.05 operations/hour. HRL 3 operations/hour, MFE 3 operations/hour, AMA 3 operations/hour, and CRP 3 operations/hour tie. Another tie covers LFT 2 operations/hour, AEX 2 operations/hour, LRD 2 operations/hour, BRO 2 operations/hour, GRK 2 operations/hour, ABI 2 operations/hour, LCH 2 operations/hour, CLL 2 operations/hour, ACT 2 operations/hour, TYR 2 operations/hour, SJT 2 operations/hour, LAW 2 operations/hour, TXK 2 operations/hour, GGG 2 operations/hour, SWO 2 operations/hour, and BPT 2 operations/hour. The lowest tied group is MLU 1 operations/hour, FSM 1 operations/hour, SPS 1 operations/hour, and VCT 1 operations/hour.

These figures are observed. They are not a forecast, an annualized full-year statistic, or an investment score. Equal values are ties; consecutive positions the rank tool assigned inside a tie are not a further ordering. Coverage of airline departures is uneven across this list, and several peaks, including IAH, describe only a partial slice of departures.

- *Inference*: Because coverage is uneven, gaps in this order, especially DFW versus IAH, are not a like-for-like comparison of total scheduled peaks. The order does not establish unmet demand, terminal capacity, or which airport is the stronger modernization investment. [F12, F19]

**Assumptions**
- West South Central is the Census division of that name. Entity resolution matched that division exactly.
- The full requested calendar year is outside On-Time coverage, so the rank uses September through December of that year, the covered slice, with no annualization.
- Airports are ordered by the observed peak-hour metric. Relative points produced by the rank tool are a calculated rescaling within the set and are not treated as the result. Equal metric values are ties.

**Methodology**
- `peak_hour_ops_p95`: 95th percentile over (date, local hour) of scheduled arrivals+departures, reporting carriers, operated flights

**Caveats and limitations**
- Scheduled peak from On-Time is a lower bound on total scheduled operations (reporting carriers only).
- On-Time covers domestic flights of reporting carriers only (no international, no small carriers).
- On-Time covers less than 70% of this airport's airline departures; delay/peak figures describe a partial slice.
- January through August of the requested year are missing from On-Time coverage, so a full-year peak-hour percentile cannot be computed. The full-year order is unknown.
- The series excludes international flights and small carriers, and it is not a tower-operation count.
- On-Time coverage of airline departures is incomplete for every airport in the rank and is much thinner for IAH and for several smaller airports than for DFW. Those thinner series describe a partial slice, so their percentiles can move if the missing departures were included.
- Division airports with no series for this metric, including AFW, FTW, HRO, and HOT, plus many smaller airports, are omitted. Omission means the metric is missing, not that a peak was measured.
- Terminal capacity, unmet demand, fares, and profitability are not measured. This is a metric rank, not a screening valuation.
