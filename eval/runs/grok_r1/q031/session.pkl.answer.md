## ILM, HVN, SBN, PGD and PVU lead small-hub passenger CAGR from 2024 to 2025, with pooled NPIAS need of $104 (estimate) per CY2025 enplanement.

1. ILM, Wilmington International, 25.3%
2. HVN, Tweed New Haven, 25.2%
3. SBN, South Bend International, 21.2%
4. PGD, Punta Gorda, 19.2%
5. PVU, Provo Municipal, 16.9%

The measure on each row is CAGR over 1 years of [Σ passengers on departing passenger/combi segments]: 2025-01…2025-12 vs 2024-01…2024-12. ATW is the next airport in the same ranking, at 16.4%, below PVU at 16.9%, so the cutoff is not a tie.

For ILM, HVN, SBN, PGD and PVU taken together, estimated NPIAS development need per CY2025 enplanement is $104 (estimate). Group estimated NPIAS need is $401.3M (estimate). Group observed ACAIS enplanements are 3.87M enplanements. The catalog aggregate divides summed need by summed enplanements (NPIAS 5-yr need / CY enplanements (window applies to enplanements)).

**Methodology**
- `npias_need_per_enplanement`: NPIAS 5-yr need / CY enplanements (window applies to enplanements)
- `cagr:passengers`: CAGR over 1 years of [Σ passengers on departing passenger/combi segments]: 2025-01…2025-12 vs 2024-01…2024-12
- `npias_need_usd`: NPIAS 2025-2029 development estimate
- `acais_enplanements`: FAA official calendar-year revenue passenger boardings
- `passengers`: Σ passengers on departing passenger/combi segments

**Caveats and limitations**
- NPIAS figures are sponsor-reported 5-year AIP-eligible NEED estimates, not funding.
- T-100 passengers are segment enplanements (connecting passengers count at each enplanement).
- T-100 passengers are segment enplanements, so a connecting passenger counts at each departure. The NPIAS ratio uses ACAIS calendar-year boardings. Growth and the per-enplanement denominator are different series.
- NPIAS values are sponsor-reported AIP-eligible need estimates, not grants awarded and not a valuation. Unmet demand, terminal capacity, fares and profitability are not measured.
- Airports without positive passenger totals in either calendar year would drop out of the CAGR ranking. No small-hub airport was unscored in this run.
- This is a growth ranking plus a need ratio. It is not the investment screening model and it is not a recommendation to fund these airports.
