**Methodology**
- `npias_need_per_enplanement`: NPIAS 5-yr need / CY enplanements (window applies to enplanements)
- `rank_score`: Σ weight × minmax_in_set-scaled criterion; criteria: npias_need_per_enplanement (higher_better, w=1.0)
- `acais_enplanements`: FAA official calendar-year revenue passenger boardings
- `npias_need_usd`: NPIAS 2025-2029 development estimate

**Caveats and limitations**
- NPIAS figures are sponsor-reported 5-year AIP-eligible NEED estimates, not funding.
- Scores are relative to the airports in this set; adding/removing airports changes them.
- NPIAS amounts are sponsor-reported five-year AIP-eligible need estimates, not funding awarded and not a valuation.
- Need per enplanement does not measure unmet demand, terminal capacity, fares, or profitability. Those are not in this data.
- The ratio pairs a multi-year need estimate with one calendar year of enplanements, so it is not an annualized capital cost and it would change if another enplanement year were used.
- Rank scores are relative to the airports in this set and change if airports are added or removed.
- BED cannot be ordered on this metric for CY2025 because enplanements for that year are missing, even though a need estimate exists and the airport cleared the commercial floor on its latest enplanement year.
