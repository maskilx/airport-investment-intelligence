**Methodology**
- `trend_slope:long_haul_share(min_miles=3000,scope=passenger,weight=passengers)`: OLS slope of monthly long_haul_share(min_miles=3000,scope=passenger,weight=passengers), 2024-01…2026-07
- `long_haul_share(min_miles=3000,scope=passenger,weight=passengers)`: share of departing passengers on segments ≥ 3000 statute miles, scope = passenger
- `departures`: Σ departures performed, passenger/combi aircraft
- `trend_slope:departures`: OLS slope of monthly departures, 2024-01…2026-07
- `yoy:long_haul_share(min_miles=3000,scope=passenger,weight=passengers)`: 2026-07 vs 2025-07
- `yoy:departures`: 2026-07 vs 2025-07

**Caveats and limitations**
- Monthly series include seasonality; slope is descriptive.
- A seat-weighted long-haul share is not a catalog metric. Weights on long_haul_share are passengers, departures, or freight. Seat share as a share of seats cannot be established here.
- Passenger departures are not enplaned passengers, and they exclude cargo-only departures.
- International seat share is a different metric and is not this series.
- The linear slope includes seasonality and is descriptive. It is not a seasonally adjusted trend and not a forecast.
- The T-100 window in this catalog ends with Passenger departures — month=2026-07 — 2024-01…2026-07. Later months are out of coverage.
- Only this passenger definition of long-haul is reported. Another distance threshold, or a freight or all-traffic scope, would be a different series.
- Unmet demand, terminal capacity, fares, and profitability are not measured.
- This is a description of activity, not a screening rank and not a valuation.
