- *Fact*: SAN's observed open-runway count is 1 runways. The ranked airports are the towered US airports with that same open-runway count. [F398]
- *Fact*: PGL's observed open-runway count is 1 runways. [F723]
- *Fact*: In CY 2025, observed tower operations are 233,275 operations at SAN and 10,425 operations at BKG, the high and low ends of the within-set rank. The matching scores are 100 at SAN and 0 at BKG. [F593, F539, F625, F719]
- *Fact*: Over 2025-01…2025-12, the filtered set's calculated general-aviation share of tower operations is 72.3%, calculated airline operations are 812,437 operations, and observed tower operations are 6.40M operations. [F720, F721, F722]
- *Inference*: General-aviation share and airline operations answer different questions. The share describes the ATADS user mix. Airline operations count T-100 departures plus arrivals. Reading the airline total as the non-GA slice of tower operations is not supported. [F720, F721, F722]

**Assumptions**
- The question does not name a period. CY 2025 is used because it is a full calendar year inside both ATADS coverage and T-100 coverage, so the rank and the aggregates share the same window.
- The question does not name a place. The universe is US airports that appear in ATADS, because tower operations and general-aviation share are observed only at towered airports.
- Rank scores use minmax scaling within the filtered set. Higher tower operations score higher. Adding or removing airports would change the scores.
- The cohort general-aviation share is the ratio of sums at the group grain, so airports with more tower operations weigh more than they would in an equal-weighted average of airport shares.

**Methodology**
- `runways_open`: count of open runways (OurAirports)
- `tower_operations`: Σ ATADS operations (takeoffs + landings)
- `rank_score`: Σ weight × minmax_in_set-scaled criterion; criteria: tower_operations (higher_better, w=1.0)
- `ga_ops_share`: (itinerant GA + local civil) / total tower operations
- `airline_operations`: Σ T-100 departures + arrivals, all carriers and configurations

**Caveats and limitations**
- ATADS counts operations at towered airports during tower hours, all user classes.
- Scores are relative to the airports in this set; adding/removing airports changes them.
- PGL matches the open-runway filter but has no tower-operations, general-aviation-share, or airline-operations row for this window, so it is unscored and does not contribute to the cohort totals.
- ATADS counts operations during tower hours for every user class. Airline operations are T-100 departures plus arrivals, all configurations. They are not a user-class share of the tower total.
- Open-runway counts come from a static inventory. A time comparison is not defined for that metric.
- This is a metric rank, not the investment screen, so screen flags and scenario weights are not attached. Tower-operation totals are not tied. Displayed scores can round to the same figure.
- Unmet demand, terminal capacity, fares, and profitability are not measured. The rank is not a valuation.
- Airports in the filter with no T-100 rows add nothing to the airline-operations total.
