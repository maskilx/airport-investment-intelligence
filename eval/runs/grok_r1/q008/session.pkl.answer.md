## SFO utilization is high among large hubs, and unmet demand is unmeasured

San Francisco International (SFO) has no catalog metric for unmet demand. What the data provide are calculated utilization measures over 2025-08…2026-07: seat factor of 82.1%, and the share of departing passengers who flew on capacity-tight route-months.

Seat factor on international=False segments is 82.5%. Seat factor on international=True segments is 81.2%. The difference is 1.3%.

The tight-capacity passenger share is 44.3% (share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.85), 17.0% (share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.9), and 1.9% (share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.95). The difference between the default and middle shares is 27.3%. The difference between the middle and highest shares is 15.1%. The difference between the default and highest shares is 42.3%. The highest share divided by the default share is 0.044.

Among FAA large hubs in the same window, seat-factor P50 is 80.3%, P75 is 81.7%, and P90 is 82.1%. SFO minus those benchmarks is 1.8%, 0.5%, and -0.0%. P90 minus P10 is 5.6%. The robust z of SFO seat factor in that set is 1.26 MADs.

For the default tight-capacity share, large-hub P50 is 35.3%, P75 is 40.4%, and P90 is 44.8%. SFO minus those benchmarks is 9.0%, 3.9%, and -0.5%. The robust z is 0.95 MADs. At the middle threshold, SFO minus P50 is 7.6%, minus P75 is 3.6%, and minus P90 is -4.2% (level 17.0%, P50 9.4%, P90 21.2%). At the highest threshold, SFO minus P50 is 1.6%, minus P75 is 0.7%, and minus P90 is -0.1% (level 1.9%, P50 0.3%, P90 2.0%).

Versus the prior trailing twelve months (2024-08…2025-07), seat factor changed by -0.1% from 82.2%, the default tight share changed by 0.2% from 44.1%, the middle-threshold share changed by -1.0% from 18.1%, and the highest-threshold share changed by -0.4% from 2.4%. From CY 2024 to CY 2025, seat factor changed by -1.0% (CY 2025 82.3%, CY 2024 83.3%) and the default tight share changed by -9.5% (CY 2025 43.2%, CY 2024 52.6%).

Inside 2025-08…2026-07, monthly seat factor runs from 75.7% (Seat factor — month=2026-01 — 2025-08…2026-07) to 87.1% (Seat factor — month=2026-06 — 2025-08…2026-07). That gap is 11.4%, which is 6.377 times the SFO-minus-median seat-factor gap and 2.018 times the large-hub P90-minus-P10 spread.

The annual figure averages a softer month and a fuller month. That range is seasonality in loads on flights that operated. [F309, F310, F311, F162, F167, F289, F292]
- *Hypothesis (not established by the data)*: Fuller flights are consistent with capacity pressure on some route-months, and also with airlines choosing gauge and frequency. These findings do not identify passengers who wanted to fly and did not, a binding terminal or airfield, or a profitable capacity project. Unmet demand stays a hypothesis. [F1, F2, F4, F315, F289, F293, F307]

**Assumptions**
- SFO is San Francisco International Airport, the unique airport match for that name and code. The San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont CBSA group is a different entity and is not the subject.
- Peer percentiles and robust z use FAA large hubs (hub class L), including SFO, over 2025-08…2026-07. The percentiles describe that set.
- international=False is treated as domestic segments and international=True as international segments.
- The three tight-capacity results use the catalog thresholds named in share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.85, share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.9, and share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.95.
- Trailing-twelve-month change compares 2025-08…2026-07 with 2024-08…2025-07. Calendar change compares CY 2025 with CY 2024.
- Robust z is a descriptive cross-section statistic. The run returned a z for every large hub so the values could be read. It is not a statement that an anomaly screen flagged SFO.

**Methodology**
- `seat_factor`: Σ passengers / Σ seats, departing passenger/combi segments
- `derived:difference`: difference of [Seat factor — international=False — 2025-08…2026-07] , [Seat factor — international=True — 2025-08…2026-07]
- `tight_capacity_pax_share(threshold=0.85)`: share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.85
- `tight_capacity_pax_share(threshold=0.9)`: share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.9
- `tight_capacity_pax_share(threshold=0.95)`: share of departing passengers on route-months with passengers/seats ≥ 0.95
- `derived:ratio`: ratio of [Passengers on capacity-tight route-months (threshold=0.95) — SFO — 2025-08…2026-07] , [Passengers on capacity-tight route-months (threshold=0.85) — SFO — 2025-08…2026-07]
- `quantile:seat_factor`: 50th percentile (linear interpolation) across 30 airports
- `robust_z:seat_factor`: (value − median of set) / (1.4826·MAD), set of 30 airports
- `quantile:tight_capacity_pax_share(threshold=0.85)`: 50th percentile (linear interpolation) across 30 airports
- `robust_z:tight_capacity_pax_share(threshold=0.85)`: (value − median of set) / (1.4826·MAD), set of 30 airports
- `quantile:tight_capacity_pax_share(threshold=0.9)`: 50th percentile (linear interpolation) across 30 airports
- `quantile:tight_capacity_pax_share(threshold=0.95)`: 50th percentile (linear interpolation) across 30 airports

**Caveats and limitations**
- Seat factor varies little across large airports and is not, by itself, evidence of unmet demand.
- Outlier relative to this set; not necessarily an error or a problem.
- Unmet demand, terminal capacity, airfield acceptance rates, fares, denied boardings, and profitability are unmeasured. The catalog has no spill metric.
- Seat factor varies little across large airports and is not, by itself, evidence of unmet demand. The same caveat is attached to tight-capacity passenger share.
- Tight-capacity passenger share counts passengers who departed on route-months above a seat-factor threshold. Demand that never became a booked or scheduled seat is outside the measure.
- Seat factor is passengers divided by seats on departing T-100 passenger and combi segments. It is a segment ratio, not an origin-and-destination load factor.
- The tight-capacity metric accepts only an airport total, so the domestic, international, and monthly views are for seat factor only.
- The robust-z findings carry a generic operator note about outliers. The values to read are 1.26 MADs and 0.95 MADs.
- Monthly seat factor is seasonal. The gap 11.4% is a within-year range.
- The latest complete calendar comparison is CY 2024 versus CY 2025. The trailing window 2025-08…2026-07 is the latest level. A full CY 2026 seat factor is not in this extract.
- This is not an investment screen and not a valuation.
